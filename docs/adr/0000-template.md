# ADR-0000: Short title of the decision

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by [ADR-XXXX](XXXX-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play — the problem, constraints, and the options considered.
State the facts that make this decision non-obvious. Keep it to what a future reader
needs in order to understand *why*, not *what* the code does today.

## Decision

The choice we made, stated in one or two sentences. Active voice: "trimon does X."

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Include the trade-offs we
accepted and anything a future change must not break without revisiting this ADR.
